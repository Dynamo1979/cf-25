Rod Serling

Rod Serling was the head writer and creator of the show The Twilight Zone. The show was used as an outlet for Serling's messages of social commentary, which was hidden under the guise of science fiction and horror narratives. After The Twilight Zone finished in 1964, Rod Serling continued to work as writer, in 1969 he created the show Night Gallery which was a spiritual successor to The Twilight Zone. Rod Serling passed away as a result of a heart attack at the age of 50.

Fun Facts:
• Serling wrote 92 of the 156 Twilight Zone episodes.
• He was a passionate activist for civil rights.
• Serling was a heavy smoker and would smoke up to 3-4 packs a day.
