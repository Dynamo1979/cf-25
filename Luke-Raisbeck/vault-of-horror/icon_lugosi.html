Bela Lugosi

Bela Lugosi (1882–1956) is one of the most iconic figures in horror history. He is best known for portraying Count Dracula in the 1931 Universal classic "Dracula". His hypnotic presence, accent, and theatrical performance defined the modern vampire archetype and the character of Dracula for years to come.

Fun Facts:
• He was buried in one of his Dracula capes.
• Lugosi originally played Dracula on Broadway before the film.
• Although famous, he struggled financially later in life and worked with Ed Wood in lower budget b-movies.
